About

About Rocky Hill

Rocky Hill is a town of about 13.8 square miles in Hartford County, Connecticut, sitting on the east side of the Connecticut River. It is part of the Capitol Planning Region and is a short drive south of Hartford.

A short history of Rocky Hill

  1. 1650

    The land before the town

    The area that is now Rocky Hill was originally the land of the Wangunks, a Native American tribe. Europeans began settling the area in 1650, as part of neighbouring Wethersfield, the town to the north. Because of its position on the Connecticut River, Rocky Hill became a natural port for Wethersfield and an early centre for shipbuilding, agriculture and trade.

  2. 1722

    Stepney Parish, then a town of its own

    In 1722 the area became known as Stepney Parish. It remained part of Wethersfield until it was independently incorporated.

  3. 1843

    Independently incorporated

    Rocky Hill was independently incorporated in 1843 — the founding date the town still celebrates today.

  4. 1950 → 2000

    From 1,000 to 20,000 residents

    Rocky Hill grew slowly for its first century — from about 1,042 residents in 1850 to 1,026 in 1900. Growth took off after the Second World War: 5,108 in 1950, 11,103 in 1970, and 17,966 by 2000. Rocky Hill was also once the headquarters of Ames Department Stores, which ceased business operations in 2002.

  5. 2020

    20,845 residents

    The verified 2020 U.S. Census figure, and the town’s current population.

A modern river town

Today Rocky Hill is a largely residential community — a “bedroom community” for people who commute to Hartford to the north or New Haven to the south — with its own commercial corridor along the Silas Deane Highway and Cromwell Avenue, and its own parks and riverfront.

How Rocky Hill is governed

Mayor, Town Council and Town Manager

Rocky Hill operates under the Council–Manager form of government, with a separately elected Mayor.

8 Members

The Town Council is the legislative body for the Town of Rocky Hill.

2-year Terms

Council members are elected to serve two-year terms.

Elected Separately

The Mayor is a member and the presiding official of the Council, with the power to vote.

Boards, commissions and committees

Alongside the Council, the town has a range of boards and commissions that handle community services, planning, public safety and open space. Meetings are open to the public, and meeting video and archives are published by the town.
