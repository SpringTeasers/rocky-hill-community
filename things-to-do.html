Things to Do

Things to Do in Rocky Hill

From dinosaur footprints to riverside walking trails, Rocky Hill packs a surprising amount into 13.8 square miles.

Parks and trails

Dinosaur State Park

The centrepiece of Rocky Hill. Dinosaur State Park preserves thousands of Early Jurassic dinosaur footprints discovered here, protected under a large dome, and features life-size dinosaur models and educational exhibits. It is a state park and one of the most important dinosaur track sites in North America.

Mill Pond Park

Mill Pond Park offers walking trails and views of the Connecticut River — a favourite local spot for an easy stroll and time by the water.

More information ↗

Rocky Hill Quarry Park

The town takes its name from the ridge along the Connecticut River in the north-eastern part of town — the area now known as Rocky Hill Quarry Park. A walk here is a walk through the town’s namesake landscape.

Parks and recreation

The Town of Rocky Hill’s Parks and Recreation Department manages the town’s parks, fields, trails and recreation programmes, and publishes the town’s special event calendar.

Landmarks

The Connecticut River

The river forms Rocky Hill’s entire eastern border and shaped the town’s history as a port for Wethersfield. Today it is the backdrop to Mill Pond Park, the Silas Deane Highway’s eastern edge, and some of the best views in town.

The Silas Deane Highway

Rocky Hill’s main commercial corridor — and the historic spine of the town’s shopping and dining district. The town has invested in streetscape and façade improvements to keep it a walkable, attractive centre. Local shops and restaurants here are listed on our Local Businesses page.

Rocky Hill Historical Society

The town’s history — from Wangunk land through the shipbuilding era to today — is documented and preserved by the Rocky Hill Historical Society.
