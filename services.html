Transit & getting around

Rocky Hill Mini-Buses

Available to Rocky Hill residents age 60 and older, or any person with a disability who is unable to provide their own transport.

More information ↗

CTtransit

Operates bus service through the area, including the 910 Rocky Hill–Century Hills Express route.

By car

Rocky Hill sits on Interstate 91 (exits 23 and 24), with Route 3 and Route 99 (Silas Deane Highway) serving the town.
